# Define human review outcomes and audit behavior

Parent: [Trustworthy MSR review detection](../map.md)
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What is the minimal human review workflow that turns a flagged MSR into durable validation evidence without becoming a full incident-management system? Define who may submit or revise outcomes, the confirmed concern and non-concern categories, comment requirements, evidence snapshot and detector-version capture, audit history, conflict handling when reviewers disagree, and how outcomes may inform later offline calibration without online retraining.
