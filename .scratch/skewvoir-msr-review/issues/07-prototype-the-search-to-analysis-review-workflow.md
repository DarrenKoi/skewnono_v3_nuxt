## Question

Which search-table and analysis-workspace interaction best lets an engineer discover, understand, and disposition Review Candidates without confusing official and exploratory assessments? Prototype the priority column/filter/sort and short reasons in search, the persistent MSR review summary in analysis, evidence navigation into relevant parameters/sites/FDC/quality views, Not evaluated and stale states, multi-selection behavior, and the Review Outcome interaction. Use a rough artifact for live user reaction; do not implement the production feature.
